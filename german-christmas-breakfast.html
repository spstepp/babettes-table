Christmas morning in a Bavarian kitchen

December 25: coffee, cocoa and something sweet from the oven

This cookbook was a 1956 wedding gift to my mother. She's gone now, but her recipes aren't. In Germany the presents come on Christmas Eve, so the 25th starts slow: coffee for the adults, cocoa for the kids, and a sweet loaf or cake that was baked the day before.

Every recipe keeps its original German name and number, with two headnote paragraphs, a historical note, US measurements with metric equivalents, a step-by-step method and a five-day meal grid built around the dish. Instant PDF download on Etsy. No physical book.

Sweet yeast breads for Christmas morning

A braided loaf to slice and butter, cinnamon snails, a Gugelhopf dusted with sugar, or little Danish pastries. Yeast dough needs time, so make it on the 24th and bake first thing, or bake the day before and warm it in the oven.

  • Vol. 12: Simple Braided Yeast Bread (Hefezopf einfach) · Bavarian Cinnamon Snails (Schneckennudeln) · Simple Gugelhopf (Gugelhopf einfach) · Yeast Danish Pastries (Hefegolatschen). See Vol. 12 · Buy on Etsy

Cakes you can bake ahead

A marble cake or a chocolate loaf keeps for days under a cover. Bake it before the Christmas Eve rush and there's something to slice with the coffee. Linzer cake actually gets better after a day or two.

  • Vol. 13: Marble Cake (Marmor- oder Tigerkuchen) · Chocolate Loaf Cake (Schokoladekuchen) · Linzer Cake (Linzerkuchen). See Vol. 13 · Buy on Etsy

Coffee and cocoa

Real bean coffee for the grown-ups. For the kids, cocoa, or the thicker drinking chocolate if it’s cold out.

  • Vol. 15: Real Bean Coffee (Bohnenkaffee) · Cocoa (Kakao) · Drinking Chocolate (Schokolade). See Vol. 15 · Buy on Etsy

A plate of cookies from the tin

By Christmas morning the December tins are open anyway. Put a few on the table next to the bread.

  • Vol. 14: Cinnamon Stars (Zimtsterne) · Almond Macaroons (Echte Mandelmakronen) · Linzer Cookies (Linzer Plätzchen) · Jam Sandwich Cookies (Spitzbuben). See Vol. 14 · Buy on Etsy
