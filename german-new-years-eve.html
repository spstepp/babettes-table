A Bavarian New Year's Eve

One evening, five chapters of the book

This cookbook was a 1956 wedding gift to my mother. She's gone now, but her recipes aren't. The book doesn't print a New Year's Eve menu. This one is built from its chapters: a lentil soup for luck, a pot for a crowd, a fish plate in the regions that serve it, a late sweet and a hot drink. Each course comes from a different volume.

Every recipe keeps its original German name and number from the book, with two headnote paragraphs, a historical note, US measurements with metric equivalents, a step-by-step method and a five-day meal grid built around the dish. Instant PDF download on Etsy. No physical book.

Lucky lentils (Vol. 1)

Lentils are a New Year's dish in many German kitchens. The book has one pot that can be made with lentils, peas or beans.

  • Lentil, Pea & Bean Soup (Hülsenfruchtsuppe)
  • Bavarian Potato Soup (Kartoffelsuppe)
