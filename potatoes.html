From the Kartoffelgerichte chapter

In postwar Bavaria, potatoes were not a side dish

This cookbook was a 1956 wedding gift to my mother. She's gone now — but her recipes aren't. The Kartoffelgerichte chapter of Bayerisches Kochbuch has more recipes than any other in the book. That is not an accident. In a Bavarian kitchen in 1956, potatoes were the meal — fried, mashed, dumplinged, goulash'd, baked in milk, finished with horseradish cream.

I remember Bratkartoffeln more than almost anything else she cooked. Yesterday's boiled potatoes, sliced cold, put into a pan with fat until the edges caught. Hofmann treats that as a proper dish, not leftovers. She is right.

This 1956 Bavarian cookbook PDF translates ten potato dishes by hand from the Fraktur original, with historical headnotes, US-adapted measurements, and a week-of-meals grid for each one. Instant digital download on Etsy.
