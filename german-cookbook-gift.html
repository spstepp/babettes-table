A gift from an old kitchen

Pick the volume that fits the cook

This cookbook was a 1956 wedding gift to my mother. She's gone now, but her recipes aren't. The book is split into 16 volumes by chapter, so you can give the part that suits the person: cookies for a baker, roasts for a Sunday cook, strudel for someone who misses it.

Every recipe keeps its original German name and number from the book, with two headnote paragraphs, a historical note, US measurements with metric equivalents, a step-by-step method and a five-day meal grid built around the dish. Instant PDF download on Etsy. No physical book, so print it at home or pass the file along.
